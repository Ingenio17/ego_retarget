# EgoVerse

[EgoVerse](https://egoverse.ai/) ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07607), [code](https://github.com/GaTech-RL2/EgoVerse))
pools egocentric human manipulation recordings from several labs, with per-frame 3D hand keypoints, wrist poses and head
(camera) poses. One subfolder per lab:

| lab | runs |
|---|---|
| [`trace/`](trace/README.md) | `appliance_repair`, `stitching`, `sewing` |
